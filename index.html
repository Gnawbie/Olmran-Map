Hotkeys

  • Click a room — highlight its linked area/room-group
  • Shift+click — add another room's group to the highlight
  • Ctrl+drag — pan without disturbing the current highlight
  • Drag — pan the view
  • Scroll — zoom in/out
  • Click empty space — clear the highlight

Difficulty squares

White — Low
Green — Normal
Red — Hard
Purple — Extremely Hard

Hidden on a highlighted room (would look like a target); the highlight itself glows in whichever difficulty color is most common in that group.
